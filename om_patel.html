who's typing

Hey, I'm Om. I write code, it breaks, I stare at the error for way longer than I should, then I fix it and pretend that was always the plan.

I split my time between software and hardware — Python and C# on the screen, Arduino, ESP32, and VEX on the workbench. I like projects where the code eventually has to move something in the real world.

I'm not the chosen one. I'm the guy with 69 repos, half of them forked school assignments I didn't want to lose.
